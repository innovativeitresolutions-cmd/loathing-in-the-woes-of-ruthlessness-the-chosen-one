# Loathing in the Woes of Ruthlessness: The Chosen One

**Working novel title:** *The Suffocating Shadows of Sallisaw: The Chosen One*  
**Author:** Skyler Blue  
**Genre:** Psychological techno-thriller, crime mystery, family drama

A fictional novel about a father and software builder navigating the breakdown of his family life, digital influence operations, manipulation and criminal schemes. At its heart is the relationship between Skyler, Kaylee, and their daughters **Luna Avigail, Summer Skye, and Alexis Isabella-Jane**.
