## Big decisions to make before rewriting chapters

- **Main plot:** What is the one conflict that connects the fake messages, $10 million job, trust, musical campaign, and Aegis?
- **Inheritance:** Genuine, forged, or mixed? Who set it up? Who has legal control? Where does it end up?
- **Antagonists:** Is Thorne the organizer, a broker, or a decoy? What specific crime can be established?
- **Skyler's arc:** What mistake does he make early, and what concrete behavior proves he has changed in the climax?
- **Timeline:** Does Chapter 19 release the preliminary dossier and Chapter 24 release the final verified packet? If so, label both.
- **Ending pace:** Expand Chapters 20–24 so the book's ending feels earned rather than rushed.
- **Genre boundary:** Make the fictional story visibly distinct from personal letters and unverifiable accusations about identifiable real people.
